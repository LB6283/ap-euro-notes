### Motives for Expansion
- Catholic Europe had been isolated for nearly a millennium.
	- While they had some outside contact, it was limited
- The textbook notes 3 key things which drove Europeans to explore:
	- Land
	- Economic motivation
	- Religious ambitions
#### The Allure of New Lands
- Literature and tales encourages Europeans to try to explore new lands
	- *Travels of John Mandeville* discusses lands with lots of treasure
	- Other literature discussed frightening figures, such as giants that eat human flesh
	- Some told tales of Christian kingdoms
		- Kingdom of Prester John in Africa
		- Small religious community in India which was supposedly founded by Thomas, and apostle of Jesus
#### Economic Motivation
- Most famous travelers to the east from medieval times were the Polos from Venice.
	- Niccolo and Maffeo were Merchants from Venice
	- Niccolo's son [[Marco Polo]] also came with them
- While some tried to follow in the footsteps of the Polos, Europe was cut off from the East by the Ottoman Turks
- *Travels* inspired future explorers, Christopher Columbus had a copy of it with him when he left on his voyage.
- After the Ottomans conquered much of the Middle East and the Byzantine Empire, the price to acquire spice became way too high
	- This led Europeans to try to find new ways to get these spices
- In addition, Europeans believed that new parts of the world would have a great wealth of gold and silver.
	- Motivated by "God, Glory, and Gold"
#### Religious Ambition
- Many explorers had a crusade-like mentality, wanted to convert non-believers to Christianity.
	- [[Prince Henry the Navigator of Portugal]] was motivated by the desire to "make increase in the faith of our Lord Jesus Christ and to bring him all the souls that should be saved"
- Many agree that this motivation was secondary, however
### Means to Expand
