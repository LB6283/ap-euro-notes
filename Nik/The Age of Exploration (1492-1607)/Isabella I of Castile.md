(1452-1516)
- [[Ferdinand II of Aragon]]
- In 1469, Isabella and Ferdinand married, joining the two most powerful kingdoms of the Iberian Peninsula and laying the foundation for a unified Spain
- Together, they established the Spanish Inquisition in 1478
	- The purpose was to enforce catholic religious uniformity, and thus strengthen their monarchial control
	- It primarily targeted Jews and Muslims that converted to Christianity but were suspected of secretly practicing their former religions as well as other suspected heretics
	- In 1492, Jews and Muslims who refused to convert to Christianity were expelled from Spain
- in 1492, they sponsored [[Christopher Columbus]]'s expedition across the Atlantic, beginning Spain's expansion into the Americas
